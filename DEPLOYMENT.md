# Chunk Client Deployment Guide\n\n## Vercel Deployment\n\nYour Chunk Client is configured for automatic deployment on Vercel!\n\n### Quick Deploy Steps:\n\n1. Go to https://vercel.com\n2. Sign in with GitHub\n3. Click \"Add New\" → \"Project\"\n4. Select `Chunk-Client` repository\n5. Click \"Import\"\n6. Vercel auto-detects all settings ✅\n7. Click \"Deploy\" 🚀\n\n### What Happens:\n- Vercel clones your repo\n- Runs `npm install`\n- Builds with `npm run build`\n- Deploys the `dist/` folder\n- Gives you a live URL!\n\n### Your Live URL Format:\n`https://chunk-client-xxx.vercel.app`\n\n### Environment Variables (if needed):\nAdd in Vercel dashboard under \"Settings\" → \"Environment Variables\"\n\n```\nNODE_ENV=production\n```\n\n### Auto-Deploy:\n- Push to `main` branch → Auto-deploys! 🚀\n- Every commit triggers new build\n\n---\n\n**That's it! Your Chunk Client will be live in minutes!** 🎉\n
